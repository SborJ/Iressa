# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, welcome newcomers, and keep
criticism about the work, not the person. Harassment, personal attacks and
discrimination are not acceptable here.

Iressa touches on cancer, and some people arrive with a personal stake in the
subject. Take extra care in how you discuss it, and never present the model's
output as medical advice.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the
[SborJ organisation](https://github.com/SborJ) on GitHub. Reports are kept
confidential.
